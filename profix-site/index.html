Frequently asked questions

Before you request an estimate.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. Small repairs, punch-list items and everyday property maintenance are a core part of the ProFix service mix.

Can I send photos first?

Yes. Texting photos is one of the easiest ways to start. Include the problem area, a wider view, your city and a short description of what you want done.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. ProFix is set up for rental turnovers, make-ready work, punch lists and recurring property-maintenance needs.

What if the project needs a licensed trade?

Specialized electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structural and other licensed work is coordinated with appropriately licensed professionals when required by the project.

What areas do you serve?

The current service area includes Vallejo, Benicia, American Canyon, Napa, Richmond, Berkeley, Oakland and San Rafael.
